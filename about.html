About TPMG

Property management built like a family trust, run like a business.

Why we exist

Most owners don't want a property manager who simply collects rent. They want someone who treats their asset the way they'd treat it themselves — careful with the money, honest about problems, and quick to act when something needs attention.

TPMG was built around that standard. Before we ever manage a unit, we put the discipline in place first: a dedicated trust account, a compliance calendar for every jurisdiction we operate in, and a vendor network that's already been vetted rather than assembled on the fly.

Who it's for

We work with owners of single-family rentals and small-to-midsize multifamily buildings across California — from someone renting out a first property to a family managing a portfolio built over decades.

What our clients tend to have in common is less about portfolio size and more about expectations: they want a clear monthly statement, a person who answers the phone, and confidence that the rules are being followed even when no one's checking.

What guides how we operate

Stewardship

Client funds and client property are treated as something we're accountable for, not something we administer. That shapes every process — from how trust sub-ledgers are reconciled to how fast a maintenance request gets a response.

Transparency

Owners get a consistent monthly statement, a documented fee schedule, and a direct point of contact. If something changes — a rule, a cost, a timeline — we'd rather over-communicate it than let an owner find out on their own.

Compliance first

Security deposit handling, rent cap disclosures, fair housing screening, jurisdiction-specific ordinances — the rules get followed whether or not anyone is watching, because that's what protects the owner as much as the tenant.

Standards we hold ourselves to

  • Trust accounting

    Owner funds are held in a dedicated trust account with per-property sub-ledgers, reconciled monthly, and never commingled with operating funds.

  • Licensed & insured

    Every vendor on our network carries current insurance and, where applicable, a valid license, verified before they're added to the list.

  • Documented decisions

    Screening criteria, deposit handling, and jurisdiction rules are written down once and applied consistently, not decided property by property.

  • A named contact

    Owners know who to call. Response-time expectations are set in writing during onboarding, not left implicit.
