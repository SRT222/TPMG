Owner referral program

Owners who bring in new business are rewarded with real discounts on their own management fees — or cash, if they'd rather have that.

How units are earned

Every owner earns one referral unit for each new property brought in by an owner they referred. There's no cap on how many units an owner can accumulate.

How units are applied

Units applied (per property)Discount rateCumulative discount
Units 1–102% eachUp to 20%
Units 11–201% eachUp to 30% (hard cap)

The discount applies to the base management fee of a property the owner chooses. Owners can allocate units freely across their own properties, in any combination, up to 20 units per property. 30% is a hard cap — no single property's management fee can be discounted further, regardless of banked unit balance.

Banked units

Units beyond the 20 applied to a property are banked, and stay tied to the specific referred property that generated them. A banked unit can be cashed out, at the owner's choice, any time after the referred property has been an active client for 12 months. Cash-out value is 2% of that referred property's year-1 rent, paid once — a referred property with $200,000 in year-1 rent cashes out its unit for $4,000. Cash-out is one-way: once redeemed for cash, a unit can't later be reallocated as a discount, and cashed-out units aren't clawed back if the associated property later leaves.

Discount and unit validity

A discount or banked unit stays active only as long as its associated referred property remains an active TPMG client. If a referred property's owner terminates, any unit tied to that property — applied or banked — becomes void.
