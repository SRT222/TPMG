Our mission

Why TPMG exists

TPMG exists to protect and grow the real estate assets our clients have spent a lifetime building. We treat every property in our care as if it were our own — disciplined trust accounting, proactive maintenance, and a standard of stewardship that doesn't waver between good markets and hard ones. We believe an owner should never have to choose between a manager who is responsive and one who is rigorous; our clients get both. Every decision we make, from vendor selection to rent strategy, is weighed against a single question: does this protect the asset and grow its value over time. That is the job we've built this company to do, property by property, for as long as our clients trust us with it.

Fee schedule

Priced to reflect where we work

We manage exclusively in and around Burbank — not spread thin across the whole basin. That focus is what lets us hold costs down for owners.

Working one market means our vendor relationships are deeper and our rates with them are better than a citywide firm can usually get — we send the same trusted plumber, electrician, and landscaping crews to property after property, which keeps response times fast and invoice markups honest. It also means less windshield time for our team, fewer surprises from unfamiliar buildings and permitting quirks, and a tighter, more efficient operation overall. We pass that efficiency back to owners in the rate itself, not just in service quality.

Our full fee schedule — management rates, per-event fees, the new-owner onboarding scale, and what's never charged at all — is laid out in detail on its own page.
