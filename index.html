“An owner should never have to wonder where their money is or whether their property is being looked after. Our job is to make sure they never have to ask.” — TPMG operating principle

What stewardship means in practice

Three commitments sit underneath everything we do for an owner, from the first walkthrough to the monthly statement.

Trust accounting, kept clean

Owner funds sit in a dedicated trust account with a per-property sub-ledger, reconciled monthly and never commingled with operating funds.

Reporting you can actually read

A consistent monthly statement format, a named point of contact, and a 30-day outlook so owners know what's coming before it happens.

A vetted vendor network

Every plumber, electrician, and contractor on our list is licensed, insured, and held to agreed response times — especially for emergencies.

Getting a property under management

Onboarding follows the same sequence for every owner, so nothing about the transition is improvised.

Agreement & scope

We walk through fees, term, and authority limits before anything is signed.

Documentation & walkthrough

Leases, insurance, and a dated photo record establish a clear baseline.

Systems & handoff

The property, owner portal, and trust sub-ledger go live; tenants get a clean transition notice.

First 30 days
